## Description (copy everything below)

Stop feeling alone in Spain. Start feeling at home.

You moved to Spain to start a new life. But when Friday night comes, you still have no one to go out with. Fluoverse helps expats make real friends and finally feel at home.

Fluoverse is the friend-making app for people who relocated to Spain. We match you with locals and fellow expats who share your interests, guide you through recurring meetups, and help you keep showing up until someone becomes a real friend. Spanish and culture coaching are built in so you have the words and confidence when it matters.

Meet people who share your interests

Get matched through a trustworthy process based on what you care about. Skip awkward events where you sit in a corner alone. Do activities with people you actually want to see again, from padel and running groups to language exchanges and neighbourhood meetups.

Build friendships that last

One-off meetups rarely turn into real friends. Fluoverse is built for continuous interaction: recurring group activities with the same people, week after week, until strangers become the friends you call on a Saturday.

Go from a good evening to a real friendship

We help you follow up, make plans, and stay in touch between meetups. The goal is not another hello. It is people you go out with on the weekend.

Speak confidently when you meet people

Once you have someone to meet, you need the words. Practice ordering at a café, introducing yourself, joining a group conversation, or asking someone to meet again. Speak out loud in situations you will face this week. Get real-time feedback without stopping the conversation.

Understand how Spain actually works

Friendships here have their own rhythm. Learn why plans happen last-minute on WhatsApp, how to small talk, joke, and fit in so you stop feeling like an outsider in the room. Culture coaching supports the friendships you are building, not textbook drills.

Join live speaking practice

Practice with learners from around the world who are also building a life abroad. No scripts, no pressure. Extra conversation time before a social event, when you want it.

Built for your first months in Spain

Whether you are in Madrid, Valencia, Barcelona, or anywhere in Spain, Fluoverse helps you build a social circle from day one. Real friends first. The Spanish and culture you need come along the way.

Coming soon: real-world missions

Meet people through language challenges in partner cafés, shops, and coworking spaces in your city, turning new connections into live experience.

Fluoverse is not about perfect grammar. It is about having people who feel like family in your new country.
