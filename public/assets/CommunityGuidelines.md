# COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

**Last updated July 30, 2026**

---

Fluoverse helps you practice languages and meet people in real life. These guidelines apply when you use community features in the Fluoverse app — including events, chats, invites, and profiles.

By joining Fluoverse community spaces, you agree to follow these guidelines.

---

## 1. BE WELCOMING

- Treat everyone with respect — locals, newcomers, and first-timers alike.
- Language practice can feel vulnerable. Be patient, encouraging, and inclusive.
- Assume good intent. Many people are practicing a language that is not their first.

---

## 2. SHOW UP KINDLY AT EVENTS

- If you Join an event, plan to attend. If plans change, cancel in the app before the event starts.
- Arrive on time when you can, and look for the Fluoverse or language-exchange group.
- Say hello if you're new — hosts and members are usually happy to welcome you.
- Follow the venue's rules and the host's instructions on the day.

---

## 3. KEEP IT SAFE AND APPROPRIATE

- No harassment, hate speech, discrimination, stalking, or unwanted advances.
- No sexual content, threats, scams, or spam in chats, profiles, or event spaces.
- Do not share someone else's private information without their permission.
- Meet people you know from Fluoverse in public places when possible, especially the first time.

---

## 4. EVENTS ON FLUOVERSE

- Some events are Fluoverse partner events. Others are curated listings from Meetup, Luma, or similar platforms.
- For listed Meetup/Luma events, register on the official page as well when the app asks you to.
- Event details (time, place, host) can change. Double-check before you go.
- Fluoverse vets listings for quality, but we do not control every third-party host or venue.

---

## 5. IF PLANS CHANGE

- Free to leave anytime in the app before the event starts.
- If a host cancels, we will notify you when we can.
- Do not ghost a host or group when you already said you were going — cancel early so someone else can take your spot.

---

## 6. REPORTING PROBLEMS

- If something feels unsafe, inappropriate, or off, report the event or message us.
- Use **Report** on an event in the app, or email [operations@fluoverse.com](mailto:operations@fluoverse.com).
- We review reports and may remove content, restrict accounts, or take other action when needed.

---

## 7. WHAT WE MAY DO

We may remove content, cancel RSVPs, restrict community features, or suspend accounts that break these guidelines or our [Terms & Conditions](/terms).

Serious violations (harassment, threats, illegal activity) may lead to immediate removal.

---

Questions about these guidelines or a community experience?
