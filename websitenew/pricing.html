Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about learning Spanish with Fluoverse

How does the free trial work?

+

Start with a 7-day free trial - no credit card required. Experience daily Fluency Rooms, AI feedback, and all features. Cancel anytime during the trial with no charges.

What if I'm not satisfied?

+

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not speaking Spanish with more confidence after 30 days, we'll refund your payment in full.

How often are Fluency Rooms available?

+

Daily Fluency Rooms run throughout the day with different time slots. You can join as many as you want - no limits on practice time.

Do I need to know any Spanish to start?

+

No prior Spanish knowledge needed! Our AI coach guides you through every conversation, and you'll be speaking basic Spanish within your first session.

Can I cancel anytime?

+

Yes, cancel anytime from your account dashboard. Your access continues until the end of your billing period, so you get the full value you paid for.

Is there a mobile app?

+

Yes! Access Fluency Rooms, practice conversations, and track your progress on our mobile app. Works offline too for when you're on the go.
